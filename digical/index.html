About DiGiCAL

DiGiCAL is built by TruApps Studio with a focus on delivering a premium calculator experience without unnecessary complexity.

The application combines the familiar feeling of traditional calculators with a modern, responsive interface designed for everyday use.

Whether you're calculating everyday expenses, percentages, taxes, or scientific expressions, DiGiCAL is designed to be reliable, fast, and enjoyable to use.
